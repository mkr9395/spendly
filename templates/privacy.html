Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and a password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to authenticate you, to record and display your expenses, and to generate summaries and filters. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our application database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. We keep it for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and expense data will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please contact the Spendly team through the support email provided on the site.
